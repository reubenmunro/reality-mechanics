# P-015 — Carrying through transformation investigation

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: ungrounded investigation

Structural radius: dependency

## Participant opening

The participant proposed:

> Nothing ever lost, nothing ever gained.

This arose while asking whether scale resolves back on itself and whether Space is found at a recurring scale. The participant then clarified that the question concerned conservation rather than repetition.

The opening is retained in the participant's language. It is not yet promoted as an Atlas determination.

## Question

Can apparent gain and loss be placed as bounded reads of transformation across scale without claiming that every form, identity, structure, or trace is preserved?

## Provisional read

A gain or loss can be real within a selected [[Closure Scope]], [[Reference Frame]], and [[Scale]].

A form may arise or cease as a readable arrangement. A room can be destroyed as a room even where its material, effects, and consequences continue through other relations.

Where held connection remains available, a wider retrace may show that what appeared locally as addition or removal was carried through changed arrangement. This does not recover the prior form as the same form. It recovers a path through the change.

The provisional distinction is:

> The whole may carry transformation without preserving every form.

“The whole” remains opening language here. The Atlas has not determined an absolute whole, final scale, or view from outside all closure scopes.

## Existing terms

### Fold

[[Fold]] already requires existing structure to remain carried and retraceable while becoming less exposed.

Fold therefore cannot name destruction, disappearance, or any transition in which the relevant trace no longer remains available.

### Unfold

[[Unfold]] already names carried structure becoming newly exposed and readable.

Unfold therefore does not require that the exposed structure has just been created. It also does not prove that nothing new was established locally through the exposure.

### Change

[[Change]] names readable difference across traversal.

Change preserves the path required to read one state against another. It does not by itself determine what is conserved through the change.

### Form

[[Form]] names readable arrangement at closure scope.

A form may cease to be readable as that arrangement while materials, effects, conditions, or consequences continue. Persistence of constituents does not preserve identity of form.

### Carrying

[[Carrying]] names held forward availability being enacted.

Carrying does not currently guarantee persistence of form, identity, readability, information, or trace through every possible transition. Where the connection no longer remains held, the Atlas cannot infer continued carrying merely to protect a conservation claim.

### Transforming

[[Transforming]] names participation carried through change of form, function, condition, or arrangement while remaining traceable through what changed.

Transforming is the closest existing placement for carrying through changed form. It remains a placement mode, not a universal law of conservation.

### Scale

[[Scale]] already states that order may recur across scale while each recurrence resolves through a different band.

Growth and decay are therefore local reads within a placed scale and reference frame. Increase in one band and decrease in another do not cancel into an absolute balance unless a trace demonstrates that relation.

### Space

[[Space]] names held clearance as separation and extent.

Space may recur as a readable relation across different scale bands. Each occurrence resolves through different placed conditions. This is recurrence of order, not return of the same space.

### Translation

[[Translation]] carries readable distinction across changed medium or local condition while retaining compatibility and trace.

Translation does not preserve everything. It is present only where the relevant distinction survives the change and remains retraceable. What becomes newly available in another medium is a local relation established through the translation, not proof of absolute gain.

## Boundaries

This investigation does not determine that:

- every form, identity, structure, pattern, or item of information is preserved
- every change remains retraceable
- Fold and Unfold are exact inverses
- recurrence across scale is return to an identical state
- growth and decay cancel across all reference frames
- physical conservation laws can be generalised into one metaphysical law
- an absolute whole or final scale has been established
- loss is unreal within a bounded cut

“Nothing ever lost, nothing ever gained” remains a participant proposition under investigation. The Atlas can presently support the narrower statement that gain and loss are placed reads whose wider carrying relation must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## Falsification tests

1. **Destroyed form:** Can the prior form genuinely cease while a trace through its transformation remains? If not, the read collapses form into material persistence.
2. **Broken trace:** Can continued carrying be claimed where no held connection remains available backward? If so, the proposal overrides Trace rather than answering to it.
3. **Cross-scale substitution:** Can apparent recurrence hide a changed operational order? If not, the proposal collapses resolution bands into sameness.
4. **Local gain and loss:** Can growth in one placed scale coexist with decay in another without requiring an absolute cancellation?
5. **Translation loss:** Can a translation preserve its declared distinction while other relations fail to cross the medium?
6. **Absolute whole:** Can “nothing” be tested without a determined closure scope or reference frame? If not, the absolute wording cannot yet become canonical Atlas language.

## Smallest possible canonical revision

No new Atlas term is warranted at this stage.

If the tests hold and the participant accepts an exact diff, the smallest possible revision would be limited to bounded clarifications in existing notes:

1. **Carrying:** state that carrying does not guarantee preservation of form, identity, or readability through every transition.
2. **Scale:** state that local increase or decrease does not determine absolute addition or removal outside the selected reference frame.
3. **Transforming:** clarify that trace through changed form does not preserve the prior form as the same form.
4. **Translation:** clarify that preserving a declared distinction does not mean every relation survives the change of medium.

Fold and Unfold already preserve the necessary distinction and require no inferred revision from this investigation alone.
