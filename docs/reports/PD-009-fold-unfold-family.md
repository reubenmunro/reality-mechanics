## Definitions

### Fold

Structure is carried through changed arrangement such that some of it is no longer presently exposed, while remaining retraceable.

Compression, increased density, reduced extent, layering, and concealment are possible expressions of Fold. None is constitutive of every Fold.

### Unfold

Carried Structure becomes newly exposed and readable while remaining available to Trace.

The exposure does not require the exposed Structure itself to have just been created. An unfolding may nevertheless establish new local relations.

One unfolding can expose another fold. This permits indefinite continuation without determining a final layer, an absolute whole, circular return, or infinity.

## Boundaries preserved

- Fold and Unfold are Second Order operations, not continuations beyond the Third Order terminal.
- Fold does not require Nesting.
- Unfold does not require Fold.
- Fold and Unfold do not describe exact inverse transformations.
- Reduced exposure does not mean destruction or loss of Trace.
- New exposure does not mean that the exposed Structure was newly added.
- Recurrence preserves recognisable continuity across changed instances rather than identity between instances.
- Cycle completion remains relative to a selected period and return boundary.
- Return is re-entry through changed conditions, not arrival at an identical prior state.
- Second Order Recursive uses carrying scope rather than Third Order nested scope.
- Higher Recursion may retain nested language because it is held by Nesting.
- Pulse remains provisional. No one-pulse/one-fold invariant is determined.

## Structural consequences

- Structure, Carrying, and Trace carry Fold.
- Structure, Carrying, Readability, and Trace carry Unfold.
- Fold and Unfold name a reciprocal comparative read without a causal edge between them.
- Nesting receives no Fold or Unfold carry target.
- No downstream term is inferred merely because an unfolding may establish new relations.
