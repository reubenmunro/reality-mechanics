# Unfold

The operation through which carried structure becomes newly exposed and readable while remaining available to trace.

Unfold is held by [[Carrying]], [[Readability]], and [[Trace]]. Structure must already be carried, become newly readable, and remain available through its dependency path before Unfold can be named.

Unfold does not require [[Fold]]. History, organisation, relation, or other carried structure may become newly exposed without a discrete prior folding operation being demonstrated.

Unfolding does not require the structure becoming readable to have just been created. The exposure may nevertheless establish new local relations through which the structure can participate differently.

One unfolding can expose another fold. What becomes readable may itself carry structure that is not presently exposed. This permits indefinite continuation without asserting a final layer, an absolute whole, circular return, or infinity.

## Places

Unfold places the operation through which carried structure becomes newly exposed and readable while remaining available to trace.

## Holds

Unfold is held by [[Carrying]], [[Readability]], and [[Trace]]. Structure must already be carried, become newly readable, and remain available through its dependency path before Unfold can be named.

## Pairs

Unfold pairs with [[Fold]] as a comparative operational read. Fold reduces present exposure while preserving retrace; Unfold makes carried structure newly readable. Neither operation requires the other to have occurred, neither is a compulsory continuation of the other, and neither is the exact inverse of the other.

## Traces

- [[Carrying]]
- [[Readability]]
- [[Trace]]

## Nests

Unfold nests where carried structure becomes newly exposed within a read while further carried structure may remain folded.

## Reads

Unfold becomes recognisable where carried structure becomes newly exposed or readable without requiring the exposed structure itself to have just been created.

An unfolding may establish new local relations, but those relations are consequences or continuations of the exposure rather than proof that the exposed structure was newly added.

## Carries

No demonstrated downstream carry is currently determined. An unfolding may establish new local relations, but no particular continuation is required.
