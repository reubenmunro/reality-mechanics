# Fold

The operation through which existing structure is carried through changed arrangement so some of it becomes less exposed while remaining retraceable.

Fold is held by [[Structure]], [[Carrying]], and [[Trace]]. Structure must remain borne through changed arrangement, and its less-exposed relations must remain available backward, before Fold can be named.

Fold does not require compression, increased density, reduced extent, layering, or concealment. Any of these may make a fold readable, but none defines every fold.

Fold is not destruction. What is no longer presently exposed remains carried through relations available to trace. Fold also does not require a prior [[Unfold]] and is not produced by [[Nesting]]. Nesting may later retain folded structure within later organisation without becoming Fold's parent condition.

## Places

Fold places the operation through which existing structure is carried through changed arrangement so some of it becomes less exposed while remaining retraceable.

## Holds

Fold is held by [[Structure]], [[Carrying]], and [[Trace]]. Structure must remain borne through changed arrangement, and its less-exposed relations must remain available backward, before Fold can be named.

## Pairs

Fold pairs with [[Unfold]] as a comparative operational read. Fold reduces present exposure while preserving retrace; Unfold makes carried structure newly readable. Neither operation requires the other to have occurred, neither is a compulsory continuation of the other, and neither is the exact inverse of the other.

## Traces

- [[Structure]]
- [[Carrying]]
- [[Trace]]

## Nests

Fold nests where existing structure remains carried through changed arrangement while some of its relations are not presently exposed.

## Reads

Fold becomes recognisable where changed arrangement reduces the present exposure of carried structure without erasing the trace through which that structure remains recoverable.

Compression, increased density, reduced extent, layering, and concealment may each expose an instance of Fold without becoming its universal definition.

## Carries

No demonstrated downstream carry is currently determined. Unfold is a related operation, not a compulsory continuation.
